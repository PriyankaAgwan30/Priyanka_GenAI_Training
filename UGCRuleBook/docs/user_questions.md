# UGCRuleBook - User Questions

These are 10 common questions that college students may ask about their rights under UGC regulations.

## 1. Fee Refund

**Q1.** I got admission to a college but later received a better CAP seat at another college. Can I get a refund of my admission fees after withdrawing?

## 2. Original Certificate Retention

**Q2.** Can my college keep my original marksheets and certificates if I decide to cancel my admission?

## 3. Ragging Complaints

**Q3.** What should I do if another student or a group of students is troubling me through ragging in college?

## 4. Student Grievance Committee

**Q4.** If my college is charging fees that are not mentioned in its admission policy, where can I file a complaint?

## 5. Ombudsperson

**Q5.** If I am not satisfied with the decision of my college's Student Grievance Redressal Committee, can I appeal to the Ombudsperson?

## 6. Sexual-Harassment Redressal

**Q6.** What is the contact number, email address, and office location of the Internal Complaints Committee in my college?

**Expected result:** The current UGCRuleBook dataset does not contain college-specific contact numbers, email addresses, or office locations. The system cannot answer this question from its available records.

## 7. Fee Refund Timeline

**Q7.** How much time does a college have to process a student's grievance about a delayed fee refund?

## 8. Prospectus Information

**Q8.** What can a student do if a college publishes false or misleading information in its admission prospectus?

## 9. Grievance Resolution

**Q9.** Within how many working days should the Student Grievance Redressal Committee send its report and recommendations?

## 10. Ombudsperson Resolution Timeline

**Q10.** How many days does the Ombudsperson have to try to resolve a student's appeal?

---

**Note:** These questions represent common student problems. The answers depend on the UGC records available in the UGCRuleBook dataset. If the required information is not available, the system should clearly say so instead of making up an answer.
